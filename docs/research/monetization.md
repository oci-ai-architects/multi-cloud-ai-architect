### Deliberately not on the list (yet)
- **AWS/Azure/Google SaaS marketplaces**: low fees (3%), but each needs a transactable SaaS product with billing integration, and buyers there purchase enterprise contracts. Revisit after a product has paying users; ISV Accelerate and Google Select tiers are out of reach for a solo brand.
- **Partner tiers (AWS Select $2,500/yr, Microsoft Solutions Partner, OCI Level 1 ~$5k/yr)**: costs and certification headcount with no direct cash. They are credentials, not revenue.
- **Vercel Marketplace integration**: built for product companies; revenue share undisclosed.
- **Udemy organic**: 37% split and $15-anchored cert market. Use only as a lead magnet with instructor coupons (97%).
- **Cloudflare pay-per-crawl**: private beta, publisher-scale economics.
