Earn the credential by shipping a design.

The AI Architects credential will be awarded for one piece of work: a product brief taken through spec, decisions, agentic views, evals and two deployments, then defended in review. It covers any cloud, because the method does.

What you submit

Six steps, in the order the method runs them. You work in your own repository, with your own tools and agents. Reviewers read the result.

  1. Take a product briefA realistic agentic problem with constraints on cost, data and risk.
  2. Write the spec and decisionsA right-sized spec and the ADRs it needs, each naming its acceptance eval.
  3. Draw the agentic viewsC4 context and container views with tools, memory and trust boundaries.
  4. Gate it with evalsSuites that accept or reject each ADR, running in CI.
  5. Deploy it twiceThe same design on two platforms, with the differences recorded.
  6. Defend it in reviewReviewers score the work against the published rubric and ask why.

What reviewers look for

These are the six areas the rubric will score. The full rubric is not published yet; it will appear with a sample assessed design before assessment opens, so you can check your own work against both.

  • Proportion

    The spec and the architecture are as large as the problem. An agent appears only where a workflow would not do.

  • Decisions that can fail

    Every consequential ADR names an eval and a threshold, and the evals would catch a wrong decision.

  • Trust boundaries

    Every arrow that leaves the boundary has an owner, a control and a stated reason to exist.

  • Portability

    The second deployment is real, and the ADRs separate platform constraints from design choices.

  • Cost and operations

    Cost per task, failure modes and observability are part of the design from the first ADR.

  • Defence

    You can explain each choice and what would make you change it.
